Wingmine

  • Genre: Minesweeper, Narrative, Single-Player
  • Platform: PC (Steam)
  • Release Date : Summer 2026

Progress through an immersive campaign following the journey of your squad, sent to defuse a region occupied by the enemy. Separated during the operation, you must reunite and uncover the mystery of a territory abnormally infested with mines.

Whether you are discovering Minesweeper for the first time or want to master its subtleties, WINGMINE includes a complete and progressive tutorial. Learn the core rules, numerical clues, and essential deduction techniques step by step before starting the campaign.
